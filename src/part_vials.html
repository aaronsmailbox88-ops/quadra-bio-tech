Dose plan

28 days is the multi-dose-vial rule (USP <797>) for a refrigerated vial after its first puncture; the tracker sets the use-by date from the date mixed and warns a week before. Shorten it if you like; nothing supports lengthening it. Keep the mixed vial in the fridge, upright, out of the light; keep unmixed powder cool, dry and dark.

The dose is yours to set and change on the vial page as you step up; the tracker uses it to flag a draw that lands far from it and to count the doses left at that dose. It never forecasts a run-out date.
