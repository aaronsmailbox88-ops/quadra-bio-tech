Hypertension Canada (2025) counts an average of 130/80 or higher, at home or in clinic, as high blood pressure, with a treatment target below 130 systolic. One reading means little; the weekly average is what matters.

Diabetes Canada: a fasting reading of 7.0 mmol/L (126 mg/dL) or higher on more than one day is in the diabetes range, and 6.1 to 6.9 is prediabetes. For people with diabetes the usual targets are 4.0 to 7.0 fasting and 5.0 to 10.0 two hours after a meal.

Heart rate

resting · bpm

Import from Apple Watchvia Apple Health

Your Watch measures resting heart rate every day and stores it in the Health app. Export it and drop the file here; the app reads only the heart-rate records, on your phone, and saves them with your other readings.

How to export from the Health app

Full export (simplest). Open Health, tap your picture (top right), scroll to Export All Health Data, then share the zip to this page. It can be large and take a minute to read; only the resting-heart-rate records are kept.

Quick export with a Shortcut (smaller file). In the Shortcuts app, make a shortcut with these steps: Find Health Samples (type: Resting Heart Rate, sort by Start Date, limit 365) → Repeat with Each → Get Details of Health Sample (Start Date) and (Value) → Text: "Start Date, Value" → End Repeat → Combine Text with new lines → Save File (or Share). Run it, then choose the saved file here.

A web page cannot read Health data live; that needs a native iPhone app, which is the next step if this becomes an App Store app.

In the trials, semaglutide, tirzepatide and retatrutide raised resting heart rate by about 2 to 6 beats a minute on average, most in the first months. A resting rate that stays above 100, or a sudden jump, is something to take to a doctor.

About your data

When you open this app signed in, readings are saved privately to your account and show up on any device where you open it. Otherwise they stay in this browser only. Nobody else can see them, including the app's author.

The tracker is a notebook, not a diagnosis. A pattern of high readings is something to take to a doctor along with the log.
